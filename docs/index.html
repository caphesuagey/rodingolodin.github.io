About me

I'm Sergey, a technical writer who enjoys making complex software easier to understand. Over the past decade I've worked with startups and global technology companies, helping teams build documentation that supports both users and developers. What I enjoy most is finding order in complexity. Whether it's organizing information, improving a style guide, or designing documentation workflows, I like building systems that make good documentation easier to create and maintain. I also use LLMs as part of my workflow to speed up large-scale updates while keeping content consistent and accurate. Outside of work, I stay active and social. I play pickleball, run and cycle regularly, and look for opportunities to get outdoors. I enjoy meeting new people, building lasting friendships, and having conversations that go beyond small talk. I'm also fascinated by psychology and the way people think. Understanding motivation, communication, and decision-making has shaped both my professional work and my personal life.
